## Divide and Conquer Algorithms
A strategy to solve a problem, involving:
1. **Partitioning**: break the problem into *subproblems* that are smaller instances of the same type of problem
2. **Recursion**: recursively solve the subproblems
3. **Combining**: appropriately combine the answers

Examples of divide and conquer applications include:

- [[4_Sorting|Sorting]]
