### Course Info
- CMPT 295 - Introduction to Computer Systems
- SFU Burnaby Campus
- Summer 2026
- Professor Greg Baker

The curriculum introduces students to topics in computer architecture that are considered fundamental to an understanding of the digital systems underpinnings of computer systems. ([Source](https://www.sfu.ca/students/calendar/2026/summer/courses/cmpt/295.html))
